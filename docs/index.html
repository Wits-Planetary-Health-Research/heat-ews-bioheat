A heatwave is daily maximum temperature above the monthly threshold (P90 by month, ERA5 1991-2020 at RMMCH) on at least 2 consecutive days. Heatwave visits run on the heatwave days and up to 2 days after. The forecast is ECMWF's, corrected to match the local record (ERA5). Forecasts beyond about a week are uncertain.
